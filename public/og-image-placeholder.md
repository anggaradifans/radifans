## How to Create Your OG Image

### Option 1: Design Tools (Recommended)
- **Canva**: Use their "Facebook Post" template (1200x630px)
- **Figma**: Create 1200x630px frame
- **Adobe Photoshop/Illustrator**: 1200x630px canvas

### Option 2: OG Image Generators
- [OG Image Generator](https://og-image.vercel.app/)
- [Bannerbear](https://www.bannerbear.com/tools/og-image-generator/)
- [Social Share Preview](https://socialsharepreview.com/)

### Content Suggestions for Your OG Image:
```
🎯 Main Text: "Angga Radifan Sumarna"
📍 Subtitle: "Senior Software Engineer"
🔧 Technologies: "React • Node.js • TypeScript • Java"
🎮 Background: Gaming-themed (consistent with your site)
📱 Include: "Portfolio • 4+ Years Experience"
```

### Requirements:
- **Size**: 1200x630 pixels (Facebook/LinkedIn standard)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Name**: `og-image.jpg`
- **Location**: Place in `/public/` folder
- **File Size**: Keep under 300KB for fast loading

### Design Tips:
- Use high contrast text (white/blue on dark background)
- Keep important text in the center 600x315px area (mobile safe zone)
- Match your portfolio's blue/gaming theme
- Include your profile photo if available
- Ensure text is readable at small sizes

Once created, replace this file with your actual `og-image.jpg` in the `/public/` directory.
